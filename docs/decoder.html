The vocabulary for Kyle’s first, seventh, eighth and tenth questions is here. His-tag, TB against LB and competitive ELISA are the three entries that complete the tenth answer. Kyle 的第 1、7、8、10 題的字彙都在這裡。His-tag、TB 對 LB、競爭型 ELISA 這三條讀懂，第 10 題就答得完整。

For "Hormones and mechanism", remember only the arrows「荷爾蒙與機制」只要記因果鏈的方向

Stress raises ethylene, ethylene does damage; ACCD drains the precursor ACC so the ethylene cannot be made. No structures needed — the arrows are enough. 逆境 → 乙烯上升 → 傷害；ACCD 把前驅物 ACC 抽走 → 乙烯做不出來。 不需要記分子結構，記箭頭就夠。

In "Chlorophyll and damage", note what we never measured「葉綠素與氧化傷害」記哪些我們沒量

MDA, Fv/Fm, Na⁺/K⁺, dry weight, RWC — none of them measured. That row is what Kyle’s fifth question is asking about, and naming five unmeasured endpoints with the reason beats naming two. MDA、Fv/Fm、Na⁺/K⁺、乾重、RWC —— 全部沒量。Kyle 第 5 題問的就是這一排， 能一口氣報出五個沒做的指標並說出原因，比報兩個好得多。

The general definitions are standard textbook material; the "in the room" line is written against Plants and Results, and describes our own situation. 名詞的通用定義是標準教科書內容；「在評審室裡」那一欄是對照 Plants 與 Results 兩頁寫的，講的是我們自己的狀況。
