The ACCD row is the fullest, because it is the only protectant that was repeated. The LEA14 row is the emptiest. Heat is emptier than salt throughout. The vehicle sits alone at the bottom, because it decides how strong every row above it is allowed to be. ACCD 那一行最滿（它是唯一被重複測過的）。LEA14 那一行最空。熱逆境整體比鹽逆境空。 載體那一列單獨放在最下面，因為它決定了上面所有列能用多濃。

Empty cells空格

An empty cell means "not done"空格的答案是「沒做」

When a judge points at an empty cell, the answer is that we did not run that arm, never that the arm did not work. LEA14 and BoPep4 under heat especially: they are on the protocol list and their results are not on the wiki, so confirm those two cells before the room. 評審指著空格問時，答案是「我們沒做那一組」，不是「那一組沒效」。 尤其熱逆境的 LEA14 與 BoPep4：protocol 清單上有，結果不在 wiki 上 —— 這兩格進房間前要先確認。
