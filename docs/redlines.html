What exists since the freeze凍結之後存在的東西

The wiki froze on 21 October. Bench work did not. If you are asked about ACC deaminase or BoPep4 on a plant, this is the ground under your feet — and the published wiki is still what we are judged on.wiki 在 10 月 21 日凍結，實驗沒有。如果被問到 ACC 脫胺酶或 BoPep4 用在植物上，這些就是你腳下的地面——但我們被評的仍然是公開的 wiki。

How to use this section. Do not volunteer it. If a judge asks about the newest work, or quotes the advisor memo, answer from here: the runs exist, they are one plate per condition with documented contamination, and the direction is not yet consistent across platforms. Then say the wiki is the standard we publish to. 這一段怎麼用。不要主動講。只有在評審問到最新的工作、或引用老師備忘錄時，才從這裡回答：實驗是存在的、每個條件一皿、有記錄到污染、而且方向在不同平台間還不一致。然後說 wiki 是我們發表時採用的標準。

The one-line version一句話版本

We can prove the plant is stressed. We cannot yet prove we unstressed it. Everything we say has to sit on the first half, and name the second. 我們能證明植物有逆境，還不能證明我們解除了逆境。我們講的每句話都要站在前半句上，並且把後半句說出來。

Where this page and the wiki differ, the wiki wins. Where the advisor memo and the wiki differ, say that both are honest about different things.這一頁與 wiki 不一致時以 wiki 為準。老師備忘錄與 wiki 不一致時，就說兩份各自誠實於不同的東西。
