Facts事實庫

Every plant number our wiki publishes, each one tagged with how far it reaches. A fact marked conflict is one where two of our own pages disagree — know those before a judge finds them. 我們 wiki 上公布的每一個植物數字，都標了它能走多遠。標為矛盾的，是我們自己兩個頁面講法不一致的地方——這些要在評審發現之前先知道。

Source for every row is our own published page. Section numbers follow the wiki's own headings.每一列的出處都是我們自己公開的頁面，節號依 wiki 自己的標題。
