Results reading結果詮釋

Knowing a number is the easy half. These twenty-eight questions ask what each number licenses, what it does not, and under which named failure mode. Every answer carries the arithmetic, the unit of inference and a two-column boundary. 記得數字是簡單的那一半。這二十八題問的是：每個數字能支撐什麼、不能支撐什麼、以及是哪一種具名的失效模式。每個答案都附算術、推論單位，以及分兩欄的邊界。

Why these answers look different. The first answer key said "one plate per condition, so the n is one" and stopped. Students and advisors both said the same thing back: that is a confession, not a boundary. Every answer here is written to the standard on the Boundary page — a claim, the numbers behind it, what received the treatment, and what the design could not have seen. 這些答案為什麼不一樣。第一版答案寫到「每個條件一皿，所以n等於一」就停住。學生和老師都回報了同一件事：那是認錯，不是邊界。這裡每個答案都照 邊界標準 那一頁的規格寫——結論、背後的數字、處理加在什麼上面、以及這個設計本來就看不到什麼。

Do the arithmetic out loud in the room. A reconciliation beats an apology, and each of these takes about fifteen seconds to say.在評審室裡把算術講出來。把矛盾對起來勝過道歉，而這四個每一個大約十五秒就講完。

Drill these against the clock計時演練這一類 The boundary standard邊界標準 On the drill page, select the "Results reading" chip.在演練頁選「結果詮釋」那個標籤。

Every statistic on this page is computed from the means and standard deviations published on our own Plants and Results pages. Intervals are Welch; detectable differences are at alpha 0.05 two-sided and power 0.80. All of them treat seedlings as independent, which is the pseudoreplication each answer declares in the same breath.本頁所有統計量都由我們自己 Plants 與 Results 頁公布的平均值與標準差算出。區間用 Welch；最小可檢出差異取雙尾 alpha 0.05、檢定力 0.80。所有計算都把苗當成獨立個體——而那正是每個答案都會同時講出來的偽重複。
