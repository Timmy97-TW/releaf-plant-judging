Everything else can wait. This order is the honest order: the stress is real, the recovery is only a direction, and here is why it can only be a direction. 其他都可以之後再補。這三句的順序就是誠實的順序：先說逆境是真的，再說回復只是方向，最後說為什麼只能是方向。

01

The stress is real, and it resolves dose逆境是真的，而且分得出劑量

Primary root 74.95, 34.77, 11.00 and 1.36 mm at 0, 75, 100 and 150 mM NaCl. Nine seedlings per arm, day 13, intervals that do not overlap. It is the one defensible row on the site — and what it proves is the stress, not a protectant. 主根 74.95 / 34.77 / 11.00 / 1.36 mm，對應 0 / 75 / 100 / 150 mM NaCl。每組九株、第 13 天、區間不重疊。 這是全站唯一「站得住」的一列 —— 而它證明的是逆境，不是保護劑。

02

Every protectant result is a direction保護劑全部只是方向

The 9, 25 or 40 seedlings in a condition share one plate, so the experimental unit is the plate and the biological replicate is one. Only ACCD was repeated independently, and its two heat trials name opposite best timings. 每個條件的 9、25 或 40 株長在同一個盤上，所以實驗單位是盤，生物重複是 1。 只有 ACCD 被獨立重複測過，而它的兩個熱逆境 trial 給出相反的最佳時機。

03

The vehicle sets the dose, not the cargo限制劑量的是載體，不是保護劑

Empty host culture carrying no protectant still causes anthocyanin at one in sixteen, and bleaches at one in ten and one in twenty. That pushed the working dose down to one in forty. It is the plant team's genuinely original, and genuinely honest, finding. 不含保護劑的空宿主培養液，稀釋到 1/16 仍造成花青素累積，1/10 與 1/20 直接漂白。 工作劑量因此被壓到 1/40。這是植物組真正原創、而且誠實的發現。

Four hours, start to finish四小時的路線

Written specifically so that no time goes into deciding what to learn next. 寫得具體一點，是為了不用邊學邊決定先學什麼。

25 min25 分

Look at the coverage figure, not the rows看覆蓋矩陣那張圖，不要看列

Go to Matrix and look only at the thirty-cell figure at the top. Three things to hold: the ACCD row is the fullest, the LEA14 row the emptiest, heat emptier than salt throughout. An empty cell always means "not done", never "did not work". 到實驗矩陣，只看最上面那張 30 格的圖。 記住三件事：ACCD 那一行最滿、LEA14 那一行最空、熱逆境整體比鹽逆境空。 空格的答案永遠是「沒做」，不是「沒效」。

40 min40 分

Open all 29 rows and read one column把 29 列展開，只讀「不能支撐的」那一欄

Skip the designs, skip the dates. Two lines per row: what it supports and what it does not. After that you know where the boundary of the plant work is, which matters more than knowing what was done. 不要讀設計，不要記日期。每一列只看兩行：它能說什麼、它不能說什麼。 讀完就知道整個植物組的邊界在哪裡，這比知道做了什麼更重要。

35 min35 分

Read the five figures, skip the cards讀數字庫的五張圖，跳過卡片

Go to Numbers. The dose ceiling, the normalisation flip, the two ELISA problems, why forty seedlings are n = 1, and where the chain of evidence breaks. Those five carry the whole argument, and all five are ordinary arithmetic under unfamiliar names. 到數字庫。劑量天花板、標準化翻轉、ELISA 的兩個問題、 為什麼 40 株是 n=1、證據鏈在哪裡斷。這五張圖是全站的論證核心， 而且全部是換了名詞的普通數學。

30 min30 分

Two decoder groups only解碼器只讀兩組

Go to Decoder and read "Statistics and design" and "Strain and protein". The first is the argument itself; the second is the entire vocabulary of Kyle's first, seventh, eighth and tenth questions. Leave the two plant-physiology groups for when something comes up. 到名詞解碼，讀「統計與設計」與「菌與蛋白」兩組就好。 前者是論證本身，後者是 Kyle 第 1、7、8、10 題的全部字彙。 植物生理那兩組先跳過，等遇到再回來查。

60 min60 分

Kyle's ten, reading the "why" each timeKyle 十問，一題一題讀 why

Go to Kyle's ten. Keywords first, then say, then why. The why explains what each sentence is doing in that position — understand the order and the answer can be rebuilt from scratch under pressure. 到Kyle 十問。每一題先讀關鍵字，再讀 say，最後讀 why。 why 那一段解釋每一句在那個位置的作用 —— 理解了順序，臨場就重組得出來。

30 min30 分

One full drill round, clock on自己跑一次演練，開計時

Go to Drill, take the 4 November deck, and say it out loud. The point is not to be right; it is to find the questions where even the keywords will not come. 到演練，選「11/4 的 35 題」，出聲講。 目的不是答對，是找出哪幾題連關鍵字都生不出來。

20 min20 分

Settle the six open facts把「要先確認的六件事」問清楚

They are at the foot of Kyle's ten. Not drill questions — matters of fact: which protectant version the plant experiments used, whether gene expression was ever run, the exact ELISA kit, whether the two repeated rows in Table 2 are a copy error. Improvising any of these in the room will go badly. 在 Kyle 十問最下面。那些不是演練題，是事實問題 —— 植物用的到底是哪個版本、基因表現做了沒有、ELISA 套組的確切型號、Table 2 兩組重複數字是不是筆誤。 這幾題在房間裡即興解釋一定出事。

Three sentences to carry in一定要講得出的三句話

If there is time for one thing only, make it this. With these three fluent, the session holds. 如果只來得及做一件事，做這個。這三句講不卡，整場就穩了。

  1. Forty seedlings, but they share one plate, so the biological replicate is one. We report a direction.
  2. The empty host, WB800 with no plasmid, reads about five micrograms per millilitre on the same ELISA. So this assay resolves ACC deaminase and it does not resolve LEA14 or BoPep4.
  3. Yes, TB medium does affect plant growth. That is exactly why our working dilution is one in forty.
Why these three為什麼是這三句 Each one blocks the line of questioning most likely to capsize the session: sample size, protein quantification, vehicle effect. And all three are volunteered — said first they earn marks, extracted they lose them. 它們各自擋掉一條最容易翻車的追問線：樣本數、蛋白定量、載體效應。 而且三句都是「主動承認」—— 主動講會得分，被問出來會失分。
