Three sentences to own before anything else其他都可以之後再說，這三句要先會

If you learn nothing else on this site, learn these. Each one is on our own published wiki, in our own words.這個網站其他都可以不看，這三句一定要會。每一句都在我們自己公開的 wiki 上，用我們自己的話寫的。

01

The ladder is real梯度是真的

Root length falls 74.95, 34.77, 11.00 and 1.36 mm across 0, 75, 100 and 150 mM NaCl. Nine seedlings a group, day six, spreads that do not overlap.根長在 0、75、100、150 mM 下依序為 74.95、34.77、11.00、1.36 mm。每組九株，第六天，區間不重疊。

02

No rescue survives its controls沒有救援通得過自己的對照

Six trehalose sets and four peptide trials. The one large effect sits on a plate whose own no-salt control failed. We say so before a judge finds it.六組海藻糖、四次胜肽試驗。唯一大的效果落在一個自身無鹽對照失效的皿上。這件事我們自己先講。

03

Nine seedlings, one plate九株苗，一個皿

The nine share a plate, so the plate is the experimental unit and there is one of it. The spread we can quote is between seedlings, and it understates the uncertainty between plates, which we never measured. The fix is three poured plates per condition, randomised.九株共用一皿，所以實驗單位是皿，而皿只有一個。我們能引用的標準差是株與株之間的，它低估了皿與皿之間的不確定性，而那個我們從未量過。解法是每個條件三個獨立倒的皿並隨機排位。

How to use this in the week before最後一週怎麼用

Read the facts out loud, once把事實庫大聲念一次

Not to memorise. To find out which numbers you cannot say without hesitating. Those are the ones to drill.不是為了背，是為了找出哪些數字你講不出來會卡。那些才要練。

Learn the six shapes, not the twenty figures記六個形狀，不是二十張圖

A judge points at a figure and asks what it shows. If you know which shape it belongs to, the sentence is already written.評審指著一張圖問你那是什麼。只要知道它屬於哪個形狀，句子就已經寫好了。

Run the drill with the clock on開著計時跑演練

Ninety seconds per answer. Most answers that fail in a judging room fail by running long, not by being wrong.每題九十秒。評審室裡大部分失敗的回答不是答錯，是講太久。

Work the four numbers until the arithmetic is automatic把那四個數字算到變成反射

The two ratios for one fall, the 3.5-fold that is also 0.8-fold, the normalisation that reverses a ranking, and the result we withdrew. Doing the arithmetic out loud is what ends a line of questioning.同一個下降的兩個倍數、同時是 3.5 倍也是 0.8 倍的那個數字、讓排序反過來的標準化，以及我們撤回的那筆結果。把算術講出來，才能結束一條追問。

Then do the hostile set with someone interrupting you再找人打斷你，跑刁難那一組

The hostile questions are not about knowledge. They are about staying at the same volume.刁難題考的不是知識，是你能不能維持同樣的音量。
