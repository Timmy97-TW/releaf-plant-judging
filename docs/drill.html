Drill演練

One rule: say the keywords out loud before opening the answer. Thinking them does not count — what has to come out of your mouth in the room has to come out of your mouth here. space opens the answer, → next, 1 got it, 2 missed it. 規則只有一條：先把關鍵字說出口，再開答案。 默念不算 —— 評審室裡要出聲的，練習時就要出聲。 空白 開答案，→ 下一題， 1 答出來了，2 沒答出來。

The ones that were missed沒答出來的

Everything marked 2 this round. Start the next round from this list. 這一輪標 2 的題。下一輪就從這一份開始。

    Ninety minutes, in four rounds九十分鐘，四輪

    It works alone and it works better in pairs, one asking and one answering, swapping each round. 一個人練可以，兩個人一問一答更好，每一輪交換。

    15 min15 分

    Keywords only — no full sentences yet先只跑關鍵字，不要講完整句子

    Take the 4 November deck with the clock on. Three or four English keywords is a pass. This round exists to find the questions where the keywords will not come at all. 選「11/4 的 35 題」，開計時。說得出三到四個英文關鍵字就算過。 這一輪的目的是找出哪幾題連關鍵字都生不出來。

    25 min25 分

    Full sentences, but only for the missed ones只對「沒答出來」的那幾題講完整句子

    Once the keywords are there the sentences grow by themselves. Read the say order on the Kyle's ten page once, then cover it and say it. 關鍵字有了之後，句子是自然長出來的。對著 Kyle 十問那一頁的 say 順序念一次， 然後蓋住自己講。

    20 min20 分

    Interrupt on the second sentence第二句時插話打斷

    A hostile follow-up does not test knowledge; it tests whether the volume and the pace hold. The best follow-ups are already written in the trap field of every card. 講到第二句時插話問一個追問。刁難題考的不是知識，是能不能維持同樣的音量與速度。 最好的追問都寫在每一題的「陷阱」那一欄。

    10 min10 分

    Finish on the three sentences最後只練三句

    "Forty seedlings, but they share one plate, so the biological replicate is one." "The empty host, WB800 with no plasmid, reads about five micrograms per millilitre on the same ELISA." "Yes, TB medium does affect plant growth. That is exactly why our working dilution is one in forty." Get those three out without stumbling and the session holds. 「40 株共用一個盤，所以生物重複是 1。」 「空宿主 WB800 自己就讀 4.5 到 5.5 µg/mL。」 「Yes, TB 確實影響植物生長，這就是我們把劑量定在 1/40 的原因。」 這三句講不卡，整場就穩了。
