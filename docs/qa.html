Q&A drill問答演練

Every question here is one a judge could ask on the evidence we published. Each carries three or four moves, the evidence to cite, and the thing that sinks the answer. The Results reading set adds the arithmetic, the unit of inference and the boundary in two columns. Read them as a shape to build from, never as a script to recite. 這裡每一題，評審都可能根據我們公布的證據問出來。每題附三到四個動作、要引用的證據、以及會害你沉船的那件事。「結果詮釋」那一類還附上算術、推論單位，以及分兩欄寫清楚的邊界。把它們當成可以搭建的形狀，不要當成要背的稿。
