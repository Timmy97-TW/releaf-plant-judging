Boundary standard邊界標準

A boundary is not an apology. It says what the measurement supports, over what range, and what else could have produced the number. This page is the standard every answer in the Results set is written to, and the one to apply to any answer that still reads as a confession. 邊界不是道歉。它說的是：這個量測支撐什麼、適用到哪個範圍、還有什麼別的東西會做出同樣的數字。這一頁是結果詮釋那一組所有答案的寫法標準，也是任何「讀起來像認錯」的答案該套用的標準。

What was wrong with the first version. It answered the n question with a confession and stopped there. "One plate per condition, so the n is one" tells a judge you know the word and not the consequence. The fix is four moves: name the unit, give the effect with its precision, name the competing explanations, and say what the design could not have seen. Each one is a sentence. 第一版哪裡不對。它用一句認錯回答了重複數的問題，然後就停了。「每個條件一皿，所以n等於一」只讓評審知道你會這個詞，不知道它的後果。修法是四個動作：說出單位、給出效應與其精確度、說出競爭解釋、說出這個設計本來看不到什麼。每一個都只是一句話。

The claim ladder主張的階梯

Five rungs. Each one costs something to climb, and most of our plant data stops at the third. Knowing which rung you are standing on is the whole skill.五階。每一階都要付代價才上得去，而我們大部分的植物資料停在第三階。知道自己站在哪一階，就是這件事的全部技術。

The four questions that fix a boundary界定邊界的四個問題

Run these in order on any number before you say it out loud. If one of them has no answer, that is the boundary.任何數字講出口前，先照順序跑一遍。其中有一題答不出來，那就是邊界所在。
