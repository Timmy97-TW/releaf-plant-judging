Deep dive五問深讀

The five questions again, this time as a course instead of a script. Each one carries why it is the question that matters, the science with its citations, a diagram, the numbers, the one experiment that would settle it, and two self-tests. 同樣五個問題，但這次是課程，不是答題稿。每一題都包含：為什麼這才是關鍵的問題、附引用的科學內容、一張圖、相關數字、能定案的那一個實驗，以及兩道自我測驗。

How to use a citation in the room. Cite a paper for a mechanism or a method, never for a result of ours. A citation supports "this is how it could work" or "this is the standard way to measure it". It can never raise our own n, and a judge who hears a citation used to prop up our data will ask for our data instead. 在評審室裡怎麼用引用。引用文獻是為了機制或方法，絕對不是為了我們自己的結果。引用支撐的是「它可能這樣作用」或「這是標準的量測方式」。它永遠不能提高我們自己的重複數；評審一旦聽到你用引用來撐自家資料，他就會改問你的資料。
